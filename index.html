Crystal Crafting

Seven refining chains, priced off Ore only — Basic/Intermediate market prices rest on n=1-2 sales each and aren't real listings you'll ever see, so they're excluded from the recommended route. Ore is a flat, guaranteed craft — 10 Ore plus a fixed mesos fee makes 1 Raw (not separately traded, so its cost is always derived from Ore). From Raw onward every tier is a probabilistic craft, not a fixed exchange — refining Raw can land on Basic, Intermediate, or (rarely) Advanced directly; combining 10 of a tier can partially fail (9 back) or jackpot straight to Advanced. Every craft attempt also costs a flat mesos fee whether it succeeds or fails, included below. Quantities and costs are expected values over those odds, not guarantees — actual luck on any one attempt run can differ a lot.

Slot Scrolling: Preserve vs Restore

    
Two ways to keep a slot alive while you scroll it. Preserve with White Scroll: pay 1 WS per attempt to protect the slot from being consumed on a fail — each fail also stacks a pity bonus onto the next attempt, capped, resetting the moment you pass. Restore with Clean Slate: attempt at the flat base rate with no pity, and when a fail consumes the slot, buy it back with a 90%-sum combo of Clean Slate Scrolls, priced here off whichever real 10%/20% combo is cheapest right now — not just the fixed 2 WS ratio. Both columns compare only the WS-economy overhead; the underlying scroll itself costs the same either way and isn't included.
