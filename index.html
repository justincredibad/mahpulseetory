Crystal Crafting

Seven refining chains. Each tier is a probabilistic craft, not a fixed exchange — refining Raw Ore can land on Basic, Intermediate, or (rarely) Advanced directly; combining 10 of a tier can partially fail (9 back) or jackpot straight to Advanced. Every attempt also costs a flat mesos fee whether it succeeds or fails, included below. Quantities and costs are expected values over those odds, not guarantees — actual luck on any one attempt run can differ a lot.
